# Sources

- Photographs (pour-over, beans, three cups, lattes, portafilter, croissant, café interior): Unsplash, downloaded
  and converted to WebP (720 and 1400 px) in `public/img/`. Used under the Unsplash licence (free to use, no
  attribution required; credited generically in the footer). Photographer names were not recorded.
- Fonts: Syne, Instrument Serif, Inter from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence).
- No starter template was used. A GitHub search for "react three fiber scroll" found nothing that fit a coffee
  site (r3f-scroll-rig, MIT, syncs DOM to meshes; not needed), so the scene, bean geometry and scroll engine are
  written from scratch with three.js, @react-three/fiber, drei, lenis and framer-motion.
